Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

Ostad Deutsch («استاد دویچ») is a free educational project that teaches German to Persian speakers through a Telegram bot (@OstadDeutschBot), this website, an Instagram account (@ostad.deutsch), a Telegram channel and a YouTube channel (@ostad.deutsch). This policy explains what data we process and why.

1. Telegram bot

When you use the bot we store:

  • your Telegram user ID, first name, username and app language (provided by Telegram);
  • your learning progress: level, lessons, quiz results, flashcards, streak, points, medals and reminder settings;
  • your subscription status and payment references (we never see card or bank details).

Texts you send for correction (and the last few messages of your chat with the tutor) are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the correction or answer. Voice messages are transcribed on our own server and are not stored after transcription. Only the last 8 chat messages are kept, and you can clear them at any time.

Payments are handled by Telegram (Telegram Stars) or by Crypto Pay (@CryptoBot). We only receive confirmation that a payment happened.

Analytics: we send anonymous usage events (for example "lesson finished") to Mixpanel, linked to a random ID, not to your name, username or Telegram ID.

2. Website

This website (ostaddeutsch.github.io) is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, no tracking and no advertising. The font is loaded from Google Fonts, and GitHub and Google may log your IP address as part of delivering the page. The level test runs entirely in your browser; your answers are not sent to us.

3. Instagram

When you reply to one of our Instagram Story quizzes or comment on our posts, we use the Instagram API (Meta) to send an automatic reply. For this we store your Instagram-scoped user ID and the number of quiz answers you gave, so that the replies don't repeat. We don't store your messages.

4. YouTube

Our YouTube channel uses the YouTube API Services only to upload our own educational videos to our own channel. We don't access, collect or store any data about YouTube viewers or other channels; we store only the IDs of the videos we uploaded.

By watching our videos you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke this project's access to our channel at any time at Google security settings.

5. What we never do

We don't sell or rent your data, don't show ads, and don't share your data with anyone except the services named above, which process it only to provide their function.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept while you use the bot. To delete all of it right away, send /deletedata or «حذف اطلاعات» to the bot and confirm: every record about you is removed from our database at once. Encrypted backups, which only we can read, are used solely to restore the service after a failure.

7. Contact

Telegram: @OstadDeutschBot · Channel: @OstadDeutschChannel
